0. STRUCTURE AND LEGAL EFFECT

0.1 Reusable terms only.

This License states reusable patent-licensing terms. Standing alone, publication of this text, copying it, referring to it, contributing to a repository that contains it, or distributing technology alongside it does not grant any patent right.

0.2 Operative PatentGrantBundle required.

A patent permission under this License exists only if an operative PatentGrantBundle identifies this exact PatentLicenseRelease and contains a PatentGrantManifest that satisfies the release requirements applicable to that bundle. The Patent Licensor must have performed the authenticated approval or adoption act required by the ECL-PL release architecture and bound that act to the exact bundle identity.

0.3 Manifest-selected terms.

The PatentGrantManifest supplies grant-specific facts and choices only within the meanings and limits this License gives them. A manifest cannot enlarge the Patent Licensor's authority, create a patent right that does not exist, turn provenance into title, or create a permission that this License does not support.

0.4 Retained evidence.

Retained evidence supports identity, provenance and reproducible validation. It does not add to or alter the legal grant unless this License and the PatentGrantManifest expressly identify a retained artifact as a normative input for a specified legal rule.

0.5 No effect from repository state.

Schema validity, a Git commit, a merge to a default branch, a release tag, maintainer approval, automated review, or repository publication does not by itself make a PatentGrantBundle operative or create patent rights.

0.6 Defensive-commons orientation.

The intended structure of this PatentLicenseRelease is a broad public direct patent grant combined with only those defensive or ECL-derived patent consequences that an exact operative PatentGrantBundle expressly selects. The purpose of that structure is to reduce the exclusionary value of Covered Patent Claims against peaceful practice of the Covered Implementation while preserving narrowly bounded defensive use of the Patent Licensor's own patent permissions against specified patent aggression or exact ECL policy outcomes.

Nothing in this Section creates collective patent ownership, a patent pool, an obligation to assert a patent, an automatic cross-bundle retaliation rule, or any patent consequence not otherwise created by an operative PatentGrantBundle under this License.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means an entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity. "Control" means the present power to direct the management of the entity, whether through ownership of more than fifty percent of voting interests, contractual power, or another legally effective means. Affiliate status does not itself expand or reduce the public direct grant in this version.

1.2 "Covered Act" means an act that, in the jurisdiction where the act occurs, falls within the exclusionary scope of a Covered Patent Claim and would require authorization from the Patent Licensor absent a licence, covenant or other authorization from that Patent Licensor, subject to statutory exceptions and limitations under applicable law. Covered Acts are determined under applicable patent law. Words used in software, hardware or distribution practice do not create additional patent-exclusive acts. A recipient's separate Independent Patent Permission does not change whether the act is a Covered Act under this License; it operates as a parallel authorization basis under Section 7.

1.3 "Covered Implementation" means the exact technology identified by the PatentGrantManifest, including only the artifacts, versions, boundaries, combinations and modification scope that the manifest objectively identifies.

1.4 "Covered Patent Claim" means a patent claim that (a) satisfies the exact claim-scope model selected by the PatentGrantManifest, and (b) is owned, controlled or otherwise licensable by the Patent Licensor to the extent necessary for the permission purportedly granted under this License.

1.5 "Independent Patent Permission" means any right or permission to perform an act that does not depend on this License, including another patent licence or covenant, a statutory exception or limitation, exhaustion, compulsory licence, government-use authorization, permission from another relevant rightsholder, or the absence of infringement.

1.6 "PatentGrantBundle" means the exact closed and immutable ECL-PL bundle identified by its canonical bundle identity under the ECL-PL release architecture.

1.7 "PatentGrantManifest" means the exact immutable manifest contained in the PatentGrantBundle.

1.8 "Patent Licensee" or "You" means any person or legal entity that satisfies every applicable initial-grant eligibility or condition in the exact operative PatentGrantBundle and exercises or proposes to exercise a Covered Act in reliance on the direct patent permission granted by the Patent Licensor. Status as a distributor, customer, Affiliate, contractor, manufacturer, integrator, reseller or other intermediary neither creates nor removes Patent Licensee status by itself.

1.9 "Patent Licensor" means the person or legal entity identified as patent_licensor in the PatentGrantManifest and only to the extent that person or entity owns, controls or is otherwise authorized to grant the relevant Covered Patent Claim.

1.10 "PatentLicenseRelease" means the exact immutable legal-text artifact identified by the PatentGrantManifest and retained as license/PATENT-LICENSE in the PatentGrantBundle.

1.11 "Sublicense" means a patent permission purportedly granted by a Patent Licensee under authority from the Patent Licensor. Version 1.0.0-draft.1 grants no Sublicense authority because downstream recipients are intended to qualify, when eligible, directly from the Patent Licensor.

2. EFFECTIVE TIME, DURATION AND AUTHORITY

2.1 Effective time.

For this PatentLicenseRelease, the calendar value in PatentGrantManifest.effective_date denotes 00:00:00 UTC at the start of that date. A grant under this License takes effect no earlier than the later of that instant and completion of the attributable Patent Licensor approval or adoption act required for the exact PatentGrantBundle to become operative. This draft does not authorize retroactive patent permission for acts occurring before that later instant.

2.2 Claim duration.

Subject to this License and the PatentGrantManifest, permission under a Covered Patent Claim is intended to continue for so long as authorization from the Patent Licensor would otherwise be required under that claim, unless the permission terminates earlier under an applicable termination provision. Expiration, lapse, invalidity, revocation or another legal event may make a patent permission unnecessary; this License does not convert an unavailable or unenforceable claim into an enforceable one.

2.3 Authority limitation.

No grant extends beyond the Patent Licensor's actual authority. The PatentGrantManifest's authority representation and retained evidence are part of the release and review record, but neither creates ownership or authority by declaration alone.

2.4 No implied later authority.

If the Patent Licensor later acquires ownership, control or licensing authority over a claim, that later event does not retroactively validate a grant for periods in which the Patent Licensor lacked authority. Treatment of later-acquired claims is governed by Section 4.5.

2.5 Unsupported expiration_policy field.

Version 1.0.0-draft.1 does not assign legal semantics to the schema's free-form expiration_policy field. A PatentGrantBundle containing expiration_policy is not eligible to become operative under this PatentLicenseRelease. A future release may support that field only after defining a closed, objectively reviewable model.

3. EXPRESS PATENT GRANT

3.1 Public direct grant.

Subject to all applicable terms of this License and the exact PatentGrantManifest, the Patent Licensor grants each person or legal entity that satisfies every applicable initial-grant eligibility or condition a non-exclusive, royalty-free, direct patent permission under the Covered Patent Claims to perform Covered Acts with respect to the Covered Implementation.

The permission runs directly from the Patent Licensor to each qualifying Patent Licensee. It does not depend on a distributor, upstream licensee, customer relationship, Affiliate relationship, transfer of a copy, or Sublicense chain.

Where the exact PatentGrantBundle selects defensive_termination.profile: narrow-covered-implementation, a person does not acquire or reacquire the ECL-PL permission at any time while a covered offensive proceeding remains pending if, before or at the instant that person would otherwise acquire or reacquire permission, that person directly or indirectly initiated, maintained, directed, controlled, knowingly caused, enabled through a transfer or assignment made with the principal purpose of bringing or maintaining the assertion, or funded under an arrangement whose principal purpose was procuring the bringing or maintenance of that specific covered assertion, a proceeding that would satisfy Section 9.2 if the person were a Patent Licensee at the relevant conduct time. This acquisition-and-reacquisition rule applies whether the qualifying conduct occurred before first acquisition, while the person previously held the permission, or after a termination event; the historical qualifying nexus to the same still-pending covered assertion is sufficient even if that person's direction, control, knowing causation, maintenance, transfer, funding, or other qualifying proxy nexus later ceased. A permission terminated under Section 9.4 or 9.5 therefore does not spring back or become newly available under the public direct grant while the triggering covered assertion remains pending. The proxy, transfer, funding and defensive-activity exclusions stated in Section 9.2 apply identically to this acquisition-and-reacquisition gate.

If that covered assertion is fully withdrawn, dismissed, finally resolved, amended so that it no longer satisfies the narrow profile, or otherwise ceases to remain pending, the person may acquire or reacquire permission prospectively from that later time if every other grant condition is satisfied. This rule does not create retroactive permission for earlier acts and does not alter a timely cure that prevented termination from becoming effective under Section 9.5.

3.2 Territorial meaning.

The grant applies in each jurisdiction in which a Covered Patent Claim has legal effect and in which the Patent Licensor has authority to grant the relevant permission. "Worldwide" language, where used for convenience, does not create patent rights in a territory where no relevant right exists and does not displace territorial patent law.

3.3 Covered Acts, not software verbs.

Depending on applicable patent law and the claim involved, Covered Acts may include making, using, practicing a patented process, offering for sale, selling, importing, supplying relevant components, or another act reserved to the patent right. The factual acts of running software, deploying a service, distributing a file, publishing source code, exporting data or operating a system are Covered Acts only to the extent applicable patent law makes the particular act require authorization under a Covered Patent Claim.

3.4 No authority to grant broader rights.

You may pass along the Covered Implementation and information about this PatentGrantBundle, but You do not thereby become the source of another person's ECL-PL patent grant. A downstream person must independently satisfy the applicable initial-grant eligibility or condition and receives any ECL-PL permission directly from the Patent Licensor.

4. COVERED PATENT CLAIM SCOPE

4.1 General rule.

The PatentGrantManifest must select one claim_scope.model. The selected model is applied only against the exact Covered Implementation and the Patent Licensor's actual licensable authority. The Covered Patent Claim set is determined from the immutable bundle rule and the claim's substantive scope under relevant patent law, not from a particular recipient's licences, covenants, statutory exceptions, exhaustion, compulsory licences, government-use rights or other Independent Patent Permissions or defenses.

4.2 necessarily-infringed.

If claim_scope.model is necessarily-infringed, a claim is covered only if practicing the Covered Implementation within the exact boundaries stated by the PatentGrantManifest would necessarily satisfy the limitations of and fall within the substantive exclusionary scope of that claim in the relevant jurisdiction, evaluated without regard to any particular recipient's licence, covenant, statutory exception or limitation, exhaustion position, compulsory licence, government-use authorization, other Independent Patent Permission, defense, immunity or remedial limitation. Those independent rules are applied later when determining whether a particular Covered Act actually requires the Patent Licensor's authorization; they do not add or remove Covered Patent Claims.

A claim is not brought into scope merely because some optional modification, additional component, alternative implementation or external combination could infringe it.

4.3 enumerated-only.

If claim_scope.model is enumerated-only, only the exact claims enumerated by the PatentGrantManifest are eligible to be Covered Patent Claims. No family member, continuation, divisional, reissue, validation, foreign counterpart, additional claim, amended claim or later-issued claim enters the grant merely by relationship to an enumerated claim.

4.4 hybrid or other reviewed model.

If claim_scope.model is hybrid, each part of the hybrid rule must be given effect according to the complete definition encoded by the PatentGrantManifest without using one part to enlarge another beyond its stated boundary. If claim_scope.model is other, the bundle may become operative under this PatentLicenseRelease only if the manifest supplies a complete, objectively executable scope definition and the legal-review record expressly approves that model for the exact candidate. An undefined portfolio-wide formulation is not a supported scope model.

4.5 Later-acquired claims.

If claim_scope.later_acquired_claims is excluded-unless-added, a claim over which the Patent Licensor later acquires authority is not covered unless a new operative PatentGrantBundle adds it or otherwise brings it into scope.

If claim_scope.later_acquired_claims is included-if-rule-satisfied, a later-acquired claim becomes eligible for coverage only from the time the Patent Licensor obtains authority to grant it, only if it independently satisfies the same claim-scope rule against the same immutable Covered Implementation, and only to the extent inclusion is lawful and consistent with prior commitments. Inclusion is prospective and does not create liability or permission under this License for an earlier period in which the Patent Licensor lacked authority.

A PatentGrantBundle containing not-decided for later_acquired_claims cannot become operative under this PatentLicenseRelease.

4.6 Combination expansion.

If claim_scope.combination_expansion is no-expansion, adding material outside the Covered Implementation does not enlarge the Covered Patent Claim set.

If it is defined-combinations-only, expansion is limited to the combinations objectively and completely identified by the PatentGrantManifest's combination boundary.

If it is rule-based, expansion is limited to the exact complete combination rule whose text and digest are bound by the PatentGrantManifest.

A combination cannot expand the Patent Licensor's actual authority or convert a claim into a Covered Patent Claim merely because the combination is commercially related to the Covered Implementation.

5. TERRITORIAL LAW, STATUTORY LIMITS AND EXHAUSTION

5.1 Territorial patent law controls infringement.

Whether a particular act would infringe a Covered Patent Claim, and therefore whether permission under this License is necessary, is determined by the patent law applicable to that act. This License does not define a universal infringement test.

5.2 Statutory rights and exceptions preserved.

Nothing in this License restricts an act that applicable law permits without the Patent Licensor's authorization, including any applicable research, experimental, regulatory, prior-user, compulsory-licence, government-use or other statutory permission or limitation.

5.3 Exhaustion preserved.

Nothing in this License recreates patent control over an article, product or transaction to the extent applicable law has exhausted the relevant patent right. Termination, suspension, restriction or expiration of a permission under this License does not reverse an exhaustion event that applicable law treats as legally complete.

An ECL-derived initial-grant policy may limit whether ECL-PL itself authorizes a sale or other disposition before exhaustion occurs, as provided by Sections 6.4 and 8.9. It cannot reverse exhaustion that applicable law has already produced, and it cannot negate exhaustion arising from a transaction independently authorized under another legal basis that this Patent Licensor cannot withdraw through this License.

5.4 No admission.

Exercising or relying on this License is not, by itself, an admission that a Covered Patent Claim is valid, enforceable, infringed, essential, or necessary to practice the Covered Implementation.

Version 1.0.0-draft.1 creates no separate have-made authorization that permits You to cause an otherwise ineligible third party to perform Covered Acts. Where an exact PatentGrantBundle uses an ECL-derived initial-grant policy, contractor/manufacturer eligibility and ECL-PL-authorized sales or other dispositions are evaluated under the same exact frozen ECL rule, including any applicable Covered Associate, Restricted Project, direction, material-benefit or anti-circumvention semantics that the incorporated ECL Bundle makes part of that classification. This fail-closed rule prevents ECL-PL itself from supplying an unstated manufacturing or authorized-sale route around the exact initial-grant restriction while avoiding a general guilt-by-association rule. It does not purport to prevent exhaustion or other rights arising from a transaction independently authorized by another licence, another rightsholder, statute, compulsory licence, government-use rule or other Independent Patent Permission.

6.5 Future downstream models.

A future PatentLicenseRelease may support identified recipients, have-made rights, sublicense chains, customer-specific permissions or hybrid pathways, but only after the manifest model supplies the exact information required to distinguish those legal paths without relying on mutable distribution history or implied recipient identity.

8.3 Provenance-only references.

If ecl_bundle_reference.purpose is provenance-only, the reference has no effect on the existence, scope, suspension or termination of patent rights under this License.

A normative ECL reference applies only to the exact referenced ECL Bundle and exact policy item identified by the PatentGrantManifest. Later facts may be evaluated only under that frozen rule and only where the exact patent_specific_effect makes that temporal operation objectively knowable; later ECL governance cannot silently replace the rule.

8.5 Restricted Party translation.

If ecl_bundle_reference.purpose is restricted-party-policy, a patent restriction may arise only from Restricted Party status as that status is defined and resolved by the exact incorporated ECL Bundle and exact policy item.

Where the incorporated ECL Schedule distinguishes an unrestricted designation from a scoped or capacity-limited designation, ECL-PL MUST preserve that boundary. In particular, an ECL `R` designation may affect the exact person, entity, apparatus or class within the scope actually stated by that Schedule entry; an ECL `S` designation may affect patent eligibility or rights only while the actor is acting in the exact restricted capacity, project, function or scope identified by the incorporated Schedule and ECL terms. An `Under Review`, `U`, `N`, omitted, unresolved, dossier-only, registry-only or score-only state has no Restricted Party patent effect under this Section unless the exact incorporated ECL License and Schedule expressly give that exact state operative Restricted Party effect. Omission from a Schedule is not affirmative proof of unrestricted status and does not authorize a publisher to invent a classification.

For an initial-grant restriction, the exact incorporated ECL rule must also resolve whether a nominally separate contractor, manufacturer, agent, seller, distributor or other intermediary acting for, supplying, or disposing covered articles to a triggered Restricted Party falls within the relevant Covered Associate, Restricted Project, direction/material-benefit, anti-circumvention or recipient/disposition boundary. If the policy is intended to withhold patent permission from that Restricted Party but cannot determine whether the intermediary's ECL-PL-authorized acts or disposition are covered, including where the disposition could create exhaustion, the Restricted Party patent restriction is not eligible for operativeness under this PatentLicenseRelease rather than being interpreted to permit an outsourcing or authorized-sale bypass.

8.6 Restricted Project translation.

If ecl_bundle_reference.purpose is restricted-project-policy, a patent restriction may arise only where the relevant project satisfies the exact Restricted Project rule of the incorporated ECL Bundle and exact policy item.

If the exact ECL rule treats a project as Restricted because of direct Schedule designation, Material Participation, a Covered Associate relationship, acting at the direction of or primarily for the material benefit of a Restricted Party, or deliberate circumvention, ECL-PL may use that result only to the extent the exact patent_specific_effect expressly selects that patent consequence. The ECL rule remains the source of the project classification; ECL-PL does not create a broader graph-reachability, guilt-by-association or unlimited affiliate rule.

Where project status depends on facts rather than a direct Schedule entry, the relevant conduct, relationship, knowledge standard and time must be determined under the exact frozen ECL rule and the exact manifest-defined patent trigger. A mutable registry or later governance output cannot be used as a substitute for that determination.

For an initial-grant restriction intended to withhold patent permission from a Restricted Project, the exact rule must also determine whether ECL-PL-authorized sales, transfers, supplies or other dispositions into that project are within the restriction when those dispositions could produce exhaustion or comparable downstream freedom. If that question is objectively indeterminate, the ECL-derived patent restriction is not eligible for operativeness under this PatentLicenseRelease.

8.7 ECL exclusions and remediation travel with the incorporated classification.

A PatentGrantBundle that uses ECL Restricted Party or Restricted Project policy MUST preserve every material exclusion, scope limitation, anti-association safeguard and applicable Independent Remediation Activity protection contained in the exact incorporated ECL Bundle and policy item. ECL-PL MUST NOT use the patent layer to convert excluded judicial, audit, legal-defence, accountability, humanitarian, remedial or otherwise expressly excluded activity into a broader patent restriction.

The trigger and affected_rights fields must be applied narrowly to their exact identified scope. No broader copyright consequence, ECL remedy or repository state is imported by implication.

A suspension or limitation whose commencement, scope or end condition cannot be determined objectively from the exact immutable policy item and patent_specific_effect is not eligible for operativeness under this PatentLicenseRelease.

If ecl_bundle_reference.purpose is anti-circumvention-policy, only the exact anti-circumvention rule and exact patent consequence identified by the immutable ECL policy item and PatentGrantManifest may operate. Nothing in this Section prevents the independently applicable Section 6 rule that a person must qualify for the public direct grant in its own right.

8.11 Mandatory-law and prior-commitment limit.

An ECL-referenced patent restriction operates only to the extent permitted by applicable patent law, competition law, exhaustion doctrine, non-waivable law and any prior standards, FRAND/RAND, patent-pool, covenant or other commitment binding the Patent Licensor. 9. DEFENSIVE TERMINATION

9.1 No default retaliation.

If defensive_termination is absent or its profile is none, this License creates no patent-retaliation termination right.

9.2 Narrow Covered Implementation profile.

If defensive_termination.profile is narrow-covered-implementation, the trigger occurs only if, after becoming a Patent Licensee, either (a) You directly or indirectly initiate, maintain, direct, control, or knowingly cause a judicial or administrative patent-infringement proceeding, (b) You transfer or assign patent or assertion-control rights with the principal purpose of enabling a person/entity to bring or maintain such a proceeding, or (c) You enter into or provide funding under an arrangement whose principal purpose is procuring the bringing or maintenance of that specific proceeding, and in each case the proceeding includes an affirmative allegation by You or by the relevant person/entity that the exact Covered Implementation, as such and within its manifest-defined boundary, infringes a patent claim owned, controlled, or assertable by You or that person/entity. For clauses (b) and (c), the purpose-built transfer or funding path is sufficient when the covered proceeding is brought or maintained as contemplated by the transfer or funding arrangement; continuing direction, control, or knowing causation after the transfer or funding is not additionally required.

For avoidance of doubt, where the covered assertion is brought or maintained by such a qualifying proxy claimant, the asserted patent claim need not have originated with or ever been owned by You. A claim independently owned, controlled, or assertable by that proxy can satisfy this Section when Your direction, control, knowing causation, maintenance, purpose-built transfer/assignment, or purpose-built funding nexus independently satisfies the preceding paragraph. Common patent ownership is not required; the qualifying proxy nexus is.

For acquisition or reacquisition under Section 3.1, the same narrow-profile conduct is evaluated historically. If, before or while previously holding an ECL-PL permission under the exact PatentGrantBundle, You directly or indirectly initiated, maintained, directed, controlled, knowingly caused, purpose-built-transfer enabled, or purpose-built-funded the bringing or maintenance of a covered assertion, and that covered assertion remains pending when You would otherwise acquire or reacquire permission, You do not acquire or reacquire the ECL-PL permission merely because Your direction, control, causation, maintenance, transfer, funding, or involvement ended before that instant. The qualifying historical nexus to the still-pending assertion is enough. The proxy, transfer, funding and defensive-activity exclusions in this Section apply identically to this historical gate.

After the covered assertion is fully withdrawn, dismissed, finally resolved, amended outside the narrow-profile boundary, or otherwise ceases to remain pending, You may acquire or reacquire permission only prospectively if every other grant condition is met. A permission that already terminated under Section 9.4 or 9.5 is not reinstated merely by the passage of time or by continued satisfaction of other public-direct grant conditions while the covered assertion remains pending.

A proxy assertion is included only where You exercise material direction or control over the assertion, knowingly cause the proxy to bring or maintain it, transfer or assign patent or assertion-control rights to the proxy with the principal purpose of enabling the bringing or maintenance of the covered assertion, or provide funding under an arrangement whose principal purpose is procuring the bringing or maintenance of that specific covered assertion. Mere passive investment, an arm's-length transfer without that principal purpose, or an unrelated assertion by an Affiliate does not by itself trigger termination.

Ordinary legal or litigation funding that does not give You material direction or control over the assertion and is not provided under an arrangement whose principal purpose is procuring the bringing or maintenance of that specific covered assertion does not, by itself, constitute initiation, maintenance, direction, control, knowing causation, a purpose-built transfer, or a purpose-built funding trigger under this profile. Knowledge that funding will facilitate or is necessary to the litigation is not by itself "knowing causation" and does not by itself establish the principal-purpose funding trigger. The same funding rule applies to acquisition/reacquisition under Section 3.1.

For this profile, merely giving technical notice, requesting licensing discussions, defending an action, asserting a compulsory counterclaim or cross-claim, responding to a proceeding initiated by another party, seeking a declaration of non-infringement in response to a concrete threat, challenging validity or enforceability, participating in an administrative validity proceeding, or enforcing this License does not by itself trigger termination. The same exclusions apply when the defensive act is undertaken through counsel, an Affiliate or another representative on the Patent Licensee's behalf.

For Version 1.0.0-draft.1, a narrow-covered-implementation manifest MUST NOT additionally supply description, trigger, defensive_counterclaims_excluded, cross_claims_excluded or declaratory_actions_excluded. Those fields belong to the custom schema profile. The narrow profile may supply cure_or_withdrawal only as available or unavailable. A contradictory narrow-profile manifest cannot become operative.

9.3 Custom profile unsupported in draft.1.

Although the current manifest schema can describe a custom defensive-termination trigger, it does not contain a dedicated field that selects which ECL-PL granted claims, Covered Acts or other patent permissions the custom trigger terminates. Version 1.0.0-draft.1 therefore assigns no operative legal semantics to defensive_termination.profile: custom.
